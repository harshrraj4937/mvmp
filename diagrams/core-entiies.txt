note : 
Critical database constraints
These are non-negotiable:
1. Every product must belong to a merchant.
2. Merchant members must be explicitly authorized.
3. An order item's price must be snapshotted at checkout.
4. Inventory cannot become negative.
5. An order payment attempt must map to exactly one internal order.
6. External provider IDs must have appropriate unique constraints.
7. Duplicate webhook events must not produce duplicate business transitions.
8. An order must retain its shipping address even if the customer later edits their saved address.
For money, store integer paise in PostgreSQL, preferably as BIGINT for aggregate amounts. Expose monetary amounts through APIs as integer-safe strings or appropriately bounded integers, and convert to the provider's required unit only at the integration boundary.
